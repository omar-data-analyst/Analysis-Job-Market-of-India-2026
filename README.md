**This project is a proactive and comprehensive analytical study of the Indian job market.
It involved analyzing a massive dataset of 10,000 job records.**

**Key areas of analysis:**

   1) Analyzing the impact of various factors, such as location, company, job type, and years of experience, on salary.

   2) Studying market patterns and trends, including identifying the most in-demand jobs and the companies that hire the most.
      This analysis then correlates these key points with India's economic trends, providing a clearer picture.

   3) A proactive analysis that benefits job seekers or those wishing to embark on an educational path to obtain a future job. The analysis       includes:
        - Distributing opportunities across the country's geography and linking them to the living conditions in each governorate and the
          nature of the work.
        - Identifying the most in-demand skills and distributing the demand across different age groups.
        - Determining the demand for each experience level (Junior - Middle Level - Senior).

**The objective of the analysis:**

  Not only to describe the current situation, but also to extract strategic insights that help job seekers and large and emerging companies   understand the "salary compass" and distribute opportunities based on location, experience, and market trends in the current period and     the coming years.

>> _I have compiled the entire summary of the comprehensive analysis into a file named "Final Report.PDF" for direct access to the analysis     results._


**The main objectives of the analysis were:**

    1- My analysis aimed to reveal the impact of each of the above factors on the average salary offered.
  
    2- To identify the preferred demographics and extract the most sought-after skills by companies.
  
    3- To understand the general policies implemented by companies in the Indian labor market.
